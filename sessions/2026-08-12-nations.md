# 2026-08-12 — Nations, the Marches, and shootable emplacements

A long session. Introduced the nation system, built a balance harness, added a second kingdom,
and put the project on GitHub. Ends with **both kingdoms unbalanced** — see *Where it stands*.

---

## What was built

### The nation system

`src/data/nations.ts` is new. **A nation is only a roster** — which towers it may build, what goes in
a gatehouse turret, plus optional `traits` that bend a rule. Map, wave table and economy are shared.
No tower was deleted; anything no nation fields simply stays in `towers.ts` unoffered.

Three nations now: **The Kingdom** (finished-ish), **The Marches** (new, untested), **The Wardens**
(a parking lot for the archer and houndmaster, never designed).

There is still no pre-game chooser. The **Nation** dropdown in the dev bar is the only way to switch,
and it restarts the game.

### New towers

- **Kingdom:** Pikemen (two men to a post), Warhammer Knight, Mounted Knight, and the Rock Thrower
  promoted from gatehouse-turret-only to a field tower.
- **Marches:** Men-at-Arms, Shieldbearers, Longbowman (reach 300), Flail Guard, Sword Knight, Lancer.

All the melee ones are the same `Swordsman` class with different numbers — that file backs *every*
melee post now and is badly named. Renaming it to `Fighter` touches five files.

### New systems

- **Shootable emplacements.** Anything that shoots has `buildingHp` and can be destroyed. Repaired by
  a hospital in reach, or by a siege engine's own crew — and **being worked on means not shooting**,
  which is the whole cost.
- **Hospitals have a reach** (210, 320 upgraded). Before, one hospital served the entire map.
- **`sweep` attack kind** — the Flail Guard. No target at all; see below.
- **Nation cost modifiers**, routed through `Game.costOf` / `techCostOf` so a discount cannot be
  shown in the shop and forgotten at the till.
- **Enemy shooters** (Bowman, Stone Slinger) that halt to shoot at emplacements, and a boss
  (**Morningstar Knight**, waves 10 and 20) that cannot be held and swings a flail.
- **City gate upgrade** — click the gate at the end of the road; +10 lives for 320g.

### The balance harness

`npm run balance` — `tools/balance.ts` plus `tools/run-balance.mjs`, which bundles it with the
esbuild already inside Vite and runs it under Node. Flags: a nation name, `--all`, `--gold=N`,
`--debug`, `--spread`. Exits non-zero if a nation marked `playable` loses.

## Findings worth not rediscovering

**Clustering beats everything.** Identical build, identical gold: defence packed into one stretch at
the map entrance wins **5/5**; the same towers spread along the road win **0/5**. Bigger than every
balance number in the session combined. A melee post holds exactly *one* enemy — spread out, each
grabs one and the rest walk past. `--spread` measures this.

**Rushing Field Medicine is a trap.** Hall at wave 3 + medicine at 3 + hospital at 4 wins **1/5**.
Delaying the hall to wave 6 wins **5/5**. 360 gold by wave 4 is nine pikemen you do not have, and
waves 1–5 are unarmoured rabble where sustain buys little.

**Wave 6 is the armour cliff.** Warhammer Knights arrive and pierce drops to 40%. Blunt has to be on
the board *before* then. For the Kingdom that is rock throwers, which need no research.

**Trail junctions**, measured: west-fork joins at **10%** along the road (inside an entrance
cluster), south-fork at **38%**, north-fork at **52%**. The last two bypass an entrance cluster
entirely — the late gatehouse is the backstop for them.

**The catapult is extremely price-sensitive.** At 200 the runs lose from wave 14; at 180 they win
with ~7 lives. It was 120, which was far too cheap for something that answers a crowd and armour at
once.

## Rules that changed

- **Melee posts refill their ranks between waves** if anyone survived. A post wiped out to the last
  man is still destroyed. Without this, a melee realm spends its whole income replacing losses and
  stalls at six buildings.
- **Wave rewards rise with the wave number** (~1.3–1.5x from wave 8). They were nearly flat while
  the cost of holding the line was not.
- **Advanced Construction 240 → 170**, **Field Medicine 180 → 110**. Both were priced so that
  researching them lost.
- **Farms raised mid-wave reap nothing that wave.** Closes the only real food loophole. Deducting
  *upkeep* at wave start was considered and rejected — the user spotted that it would make "wait
  until the wave starts, then build" strictly optimal, a worse hole than the one it closed.
- **Gatehouse turrets cost housing and food.** They were free; `housingUsed`/`foodUpkeep` only
  counted free-standing towers.
- **Walled houses** come with Timber Frame free instead of a flat +3 capacity, which had silently
  bricked the house upgrade path.

## Bugs found and fixed

- **Bombardment soft-lock.** Enemy shooters halted while *any* target was in range, so bowmen parked
  out of reach of the defence whittling at an emplacement a hospital mended just as fast — wave 19
  never ended. They now pause only to loose and walk the rest of the reload.
- **Canvas transform leak.** `drawFlailGuard` had one `save()` and two `restore()`. The extra pop
  unwound the whole-canvas transform, so from the first Flail Guard onward everything was drawn
  rotated by the tower's facing — buildings vanished, enemies appeared to march straight upward.
  There is now a dev-only end-of-frame check that shouts about any future imbalance.
- Several harness bugs that masqueraded as the game being undertuned — see `HANDOFF.md`.

## The Flail Guard, three times

Worth recording because two versions were wrong in opposite directions.

1. **Single-target projectile.** The whirling animation was a lie; it threw a flail head at one enemy.
2. **Whole-circle sweep.** Everything inside a 95 circle took the full blow at once — twelve enemies,
   ~270 effective damage a second from an 85-gold post. Far too strong.
3. **Ball-only sweep (current).** The head travels its circle and hurts only what it physically
   passes through, once per revolution. It threatens a narrow *band* at arm's length, not a disc.

Measured, same wave and spot: 1613 dmg / 12-at-once → **586 dmg / 2-at-once**, against a Rock
Thrower's 301 and a Crossbowman's 276. About twice a single-target tower, which is the right price
for catching two where they catch one.

The renderer now reads the tower's own `flailAngle`, so the ball drawn is the ball the rules move.

## Where it stands

**The Kingdom loses at wave 10–11.** It was balanced 5/5 *with* the catapult; moving the catapult to
the Marches took away its answer to the late armour and it has not been re-tuned. It holds 20 lives
to wave 9 and then folds. **This is the top of the to-do list.**

**The Marches has never been balance-tested at all.** Roster, prices, upgrades and its harness plan
are all first drafts. The plan in `balance.ts` also does not know the two things that matter most for
it: that a Flail Guard is worth several times as much on an inside corner, and that a hospital
covering the shooting line is what keeps it alive.

Everything from the "shootable emplacements" work onward is untested by the harness — its shopping
lists know nothing about hospitals covering crossbows, or about buildings being lost.
